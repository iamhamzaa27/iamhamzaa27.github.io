About Me

I am a Graduate Research Assistant at the Communications and Quantum Information Laboratory (CQILAB), Kyung Hee University, South Korea. I am pursuing an M.S. in Electronics and Information Convergence Engineering under the supervision of Prof. Hyundong Shin.

My research spans quantum information science, quantum computing, hybrid quantum–classical machine learning, reinforcement learning, large language models, and intelligent communication systems.

I develop resource-efficient quantum and AI frameworks, including reinforcement learning-based quantum circuit pruning and agentic AI for blockage-aware RIS/LOS switching in mmWave communication systems. My earlier computer-vision work includes ResNet-50-based violence detection and CNN-based human–vending machine interaction detection.
